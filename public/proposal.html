Hosting

The video bill stays small

OMS asked about AWS. GetMatchReady already streams the same video through Bunny CDN — a fraction of AWS CloudFront's rate — and hosting is billed to OMS at actual cost either way. Estimated annual video delivery at roughly 75 TB (about 1 GB of video per official).

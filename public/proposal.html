Hosting

The video bill stays small

You asked how the video hosting works and what it costs. Short answer: it's fully automatic, and it's pennies.

How it works — fully automated

  • Wired into Bunny's API. The moment a video is added, GetMatchReady uploads it to Bunny and serves it from a global CDN — no copying embed codes, no linking by hand, nothing to maintain.
  • No broken links, no permission walls. Because we host the file, it always plays inline, full screen, and resumes — unlike a Vimeo or YouTube link, which depends on the owner's embed settings and can simply refuse to play.
  • Fast everywhere. Modules load quickly from the nearest edge; most delivery is already served from cache.
  • Billed at actual cost, no markup. Tracked per organization — you pay Bunny's rate, nothing added.

What it actually costs

Bunny's rate is $0.01/GB standard, or $0.005/GB on the high-volume video tier. For reference, our own account delivered 8 GB last month for 8 cents.

The real difference: it's automated

Bunny is wired into the platform through its API. Add a video and it's uploaded, hosted, and live automatically — nothing to link, nothing that breaks.

Vimeo and YouTube are just links. They depend on the owner's embed settings, can refuse to play on your domain, and have to be managed by hand. We hit exactly that with a US Soccer clip. Self-hosting on Bunny removes all of it — your video always plays, inline, everywhere.

At your scale the dollars are tiny either way — tens of dollars a year, not thousands. The win is the automation: upload once, it's live everywhere, nothing to maintain and nothing to break.

Integration

Plugs straight into your application system

Education shouldn't be a separate island. GetMatchReady connects to your event application and assignment system (Zite) so training becomes part of the pipeline — not another thing to chase.

  1. An official applies to an event and is accepted in your system.
  2. Your system sends them to the required GetMatchReady course with a signed launch link.
  3. They complete it — real watch time, pass/fail, score — and earn their certificate.
  4. GetMatchReady fires a signed completion webhook back; it's marked done on their profile.
  5. Your assigners and event managers see who's cleared, automatically.

The launch link in and the completion webhook out are built and live today — no JavaScript for your team to write. This is the same loop US Soccer is working toward; you can have it first, and tied into assignment the way nobody else is.

My recommendation: License-to-Own. It costs the same as buying outright ($68k all in), but you try it risk-free for a year first and every license dollar credits toward ownership. Zero obligation to buy.

Prefer per-user pricing? At your current reach the soccer license works out to about $1.00 per user per year, and less as you grow. It can be structured as a flat annual license or a per-user subscription, whichever fits your budgeting.

Scope. NCYSA and NCSRA are excluded from this agreement. GetMatchReady continues to operate their learning systems, and any exclusivity covers your sports and markets only, not organizations GetMatchReady already serves.
